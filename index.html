About

Gardens that keep
doing well after handover

I lead plant selection and landscape planning at Tamara Garden Centre in Coimbatore. For every client I start with what the site can actually support, then design a planting scheme around its climate, soil and style.

The design is only half of it. I supervise installation and irrigation, and advise on soil health and organic pest and disease control, so the garden still looks right a year later.

Expertise

What I do

Three things I'm brought in for, from the first site visit to the maintenance schedule.

Landscape design

Site assessment, planting plans and layouts drawn in AutoCAD, sized to the space, the budget and how the garden will be used.

Plant health

Plant health assessments, soil and nutrition advice, and organic pest and disease control that keeps the planting resilient.

Install & aftercare

Supervising planting and irrigation on site, then setting the care schedule and training the team who will maintain it.
